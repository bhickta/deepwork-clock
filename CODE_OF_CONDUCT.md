# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being considerate, respectful and collaborative,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- focusing on what is best for the project and for the people using it,
- accepting that a design decision may be declined, and explaining why.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Accessibility is part of the standard

This project builds an assistive technology. Harassment of people who use
screen readers, and dismissing accessibility requirements as optional, are
treated as unacceptable behaviour. Accessibility defects are treated as
defects, not as enhancements.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's private reporting for the
repository, or by contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled with
the privacy of the reporter in mind.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the other
maintainers.
